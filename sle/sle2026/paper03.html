SLE 2026 paper:
Incremental, Bidirectional Text-to-Text Synchronizations with AnyText

Georg Hinkel

  • DOI: 10.1145/3806383.3815516
  • Researchr: details/sle-2026/sle-2026/5/Incremental-bidirectional-text-to-text-synchronizations-with-AnyText
  • T4B: Horizontal Transformation
    • The paper’s main contribution is bidirectional synchronisation across textual DSLs through model-to-text and model-to-model mappings.
  • T4A: Workbenches
    • The approach extends the AnyText language workbench with generic synchronisation support.
  • T1D: Composition
    • The architecture is explicitly modular, composing language-specific synchronisations with heterogeneous model-to-model synchronisations.
  • T5F: Performance
    • The evaluation studies performance for large textual documents and incremental updates.
